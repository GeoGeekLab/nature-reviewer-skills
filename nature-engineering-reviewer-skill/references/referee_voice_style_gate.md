# Referee Voice Style Gate

The final report should sound like independent Nature referees, not a checklist.

A strong referee report:

- recognizes the manuscript's contribution;
- identifies the central evidence chain;
- separates demonstration from extrapolation;
- connects detailed comments to claim strength;
- gives revision paths, not only criticisms;
- ends with a clear publication-level judgment.

Do not expose gate names, pattern IDs, decision tables or internal routing.
