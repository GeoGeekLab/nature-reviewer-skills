Reviewer Reports on the Initial Version

Referees' comments:

Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):

The manuscript presents an interesting soft robotic gripper that integrates tactile sensing with adaptive actuation. The engineering direction is timely and the prototype is potentially useful. The central limitation is that the current evidence supports a promising demonstration, but not yet the broader claim of robust real-world manipulation.

Major comments:

1. The operating envelope is underdefined. The authors should specify object classes, weights, surface conditions, speeds and failure criteria, then report performance across that envelope rather than only selected examples.
2. The comparison with the baseline gripper is not yet decisive because the baseline appears to be evaluated under a narrower set of tuned conditions. A matched benchmark with identical objects, trajectories and success definitions is needed.

Specific comments:

1. Please report the number of failed grasps and not only the successful demonstrations.
2. Figure captions should define the success metric and include sample sizes.

Referee #2 (Remarks to the Author):

The work is technically promising, but the validation strategy is too limited for the strength of the claims. Replicate devices, run-to-run variance and durability under repeated cycling are needed to judge whether the design is reproducible.

Major comments:

1. The manuscript should report device-to-device variability and fabrication yield. Without this, it is unclear whether the shown prototype is representative.
2. The tactile-sensing contribution should be isolated using ablations that compare the same gripper with and without adaptive tactile feedback.

Referee #3 (Remarks to the Author):

The manuscript could become suitable after major revision. The main revisions should narrow the claims to the demonstrated capability, add failure-mode analysis, and provide enough control software and calibration details for independent reproduction.
