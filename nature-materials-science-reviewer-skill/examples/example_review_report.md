# Reviewer Reports on the Initial Version

Referees' comments:

## Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):

The manuscript reports a new functional material and presents an appealing synthesis-to-property story. The main potential contribution is the link between controlled structure and an improved functional response. However, the present evidence does not yet fully establish that the claimed material phase, rather than a secondary component or processing artifact, is responsible for the reported property.

Major comments:

1. The material identity should be strengthened with orthogonal phase and compositional evidence. The current characterization would benefit from clearer assignment of the dominant phase, impurity limits, and sample-to-sample reproducibility.
2. The synthesis route is promising but not yet reproducibility-ready. The authors should report the processing window, yields or success rates, and representative data across independent batches.
3. The manuscript should position the claimed advance more sharply against the closest materials literature, using matched criteria rather than broad qualitative comparisons.

Overall, the work could become suitable after substantial revision if the material identity and reproducibility evidence are strengthened.

## Referee #2 (Remarks to the Author):

The property measurements are central to the manuscript, but the current presentation makes it difficult to judge whether the improvement is robust and fairly benchmarked. The authors should define the metric, normalization basis and test protocol more explicitly.

Major comments:

1. Benchmark comparisons should be repeated or tabulated under matched conditions. If literature values are used, differences in sample geometry, loading, environment or measurement protocol should be made explicit.
2. The quantitative results require replicate counts and uncertainty. Representative curves alone are not sufficient for a performance claim.
3. Several figure-level details should be clarified, including units, axes, sample labels and whether error bars represent independent samples or repeated measurements.

The manuscript is interesting, but the performance claim needs stronger statistical and benchmarking support.

## Referee #3 (Remarks to the Author):

The proposed mechanism is plausible, but the evidence currently supports correlation more strongly than causation. The authors should distinguish what is directly demonstrated from what is inferred from structural and computational results.

Major comments:

1. The structure-property mechanism would be more convincing with targeted controls or perturbations that isolate the proposed structural feature.
2. If computational results are used to support the mechanism, the manuscript should report assumptions, validation against experiment and uncertainty or sensitivity to model choices.
3. Application claims should be calibrated to the tested conditions. If long-term stability, cycling or practical operation is claimed, the manuscript should include appropriate stress tests and post-test characterization.

I would support reconsideration after major revision if the mechanistic and application-boundary claims are better calibrated to the evidence.
