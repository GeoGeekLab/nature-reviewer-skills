# Materials Science Presubmission Self-Check

- Is the central material advance clearly different from closest prior work?
- Is material identity established with orthogonal evidence?
- Are synthesis and processing details reproducible?
- Are property metrics measured under defensible protocols?
- Are controls and benchmarks fair and current?
- Are mechanism claims supported beyond correlation?
- Are stability and application claims tested under relevant conditions?
- Are statistics, uncertainty, source data, code and materials availability complete?
