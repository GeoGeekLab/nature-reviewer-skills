# Corpus Scope

- Research field: Materials science
- Time range: 2024-01-01 to 2026-06-25
- Core journals: Nature; Nature Communications
- Supplemental Nature Portfolio journal included in source corpus: Communications Materials
- Article types: research articles / articles with public peer-review files
- Public source corpus version: nature_materials_science_peer_review_2024_2026_v0_2

The GitHub-ready skill repository intentionally excludes raw peer-review PDFs and long original reviewer comments. It contains metadata, checksums, abstract reviewer patterns and a non-verbatim review-unit index.
