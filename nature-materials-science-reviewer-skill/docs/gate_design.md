# Gate Design

Gates are applied internally and claim-dependently. They are not a visible checklist for final referee reports. Each gate follows the same review pattern: claim type -> evidence risk -> reviewer concern -> revision direction.

Major concerns should be raised only when the issue weakens the central materials-science contribution or the reader's confidence in the evidence chain. Minor concerns should be reserved for local clarity, terminology, figure presentation or reporting completeness.

## editorial_significance_and_novelty

Claim type: `novel_material_or_general_advance_claim`

Evidence risk: The manuscript presents an advance without distinguishing it from closest prior materials, mechanisms, or devices.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers may see the contribution as incremental, insufficiently positioned, or too narrow for Nature-level scope.

Revision direction: State the exact advance over closest work, quantify the improvement, and align the claimed scope with the evidence.

## synthesis_route_and_reproducibility

Claim type: `synthesis_or_processing_claim`

Evidence risk: The target material may not be reproducibly accessible from the described route or processing window.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for complete synthesis details, batch-to-batch consistency, process windows, yields, and failure cases.

Revision direction: Add full protocols, key parameters, replicate batches, yield/throughput information, and limits of the synthesis route.

## composition_phase_purity_and_identity

Claim type: `material_identity_or_phase_claim`

Evidence risk: The claimed material identity may be confounded by impurity phases, mixed composition, solvent, dopants, or incomplete assignment.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask whether the observed properties arise from the stated phase and composition.

Revision direction: Provide orthogonal compositional and phase evidence, quantify purity, assign phases, and rule out likely impurities.

## structural_characterization_and_spatial_evidence

Claim type: `structure_or_morphology_claim`

Evidence risk: Structural, morphological, or interfacial claims may be under-supported by limited or non-representative characterization.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers request higher-resolution, more representative, or orthogonal structural evidence.

Revision direction: Add complementary microscopy/spectroscopy/diffraction, representative sampling, image quantification, and clear assignment of features.

## property_measurement_and_metric_validity

Claim type: `material_property_or_performance_claim`

Evidence risk: The selected metric, measurement protocol, or unit normalization may not support the claimed performance.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers challenge sensitivity, capacity, modulus, conductivity, efficiency, adsorption, or other property measurements.

Revision direction: Define protocols, justify metrics, normalize consistently, add controls, and report uncertainty and replicate statistics.

## control_materials_and_benchmarking

Claim type: `superiority_or_comparison_claim`

Evidence risk: The comparison set may be incomplete, unfair, outdated, or not measured under equivalent conditions.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for state-of-the-art controls, commercial comparators, or identical testing conditions.

Revision direction: Benchmark against closest materials and devices using matched protocols, transparent baselines, and literature context.

## structure_property_causality_and_mechanism

Claim type: `mechanistic_or_structure_property_claim`

Evidence risk: A correlation between structure and performance is presented as a causal mechanism.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for perturbation, control, in situ, operando, or alternative-mechanism tests.

Revision direction: Separate correlation from causality, add targeted controls, validate proposed pathways, and discuss plausible alternatives.

## stability_durability_and_operando_relevance

Claim type: `stability_or_long_term_utility_claim`

Evidence risk: Short-term or ideal-condition tests may not support claims of durability, stability, cycling, or operational robustness.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers request long-term tests, environmental stressors, cycling, degradation analysis, and post-test characterization.

Revision direction: Add durability protocols, stress conditions, failure analysis, before/after characterization, and realistic use-condition tests.

## scalability_processability_and_application_boundary

Claim type: `application_or_translation_claim`

Evidence risk: Proof-of-concept results may be overextended to manufacturability, device deployment, or industrial relevance.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for scale, processability, throughput, cost, integration, or realistic operating constraints.

Revision direction: State the application boundary, add scale/process evidence where available, and avoid industrial claims not directly tested.

## computational_design_and_model_validation

Claim type: `computational_prediction_or_design_claim`

Evidence risk: A model, simulation, DFT calculation, or machine-learning design may not be sufficiently validated experimentally or out of distribution.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for assumptions, convergence, descriptors, training/test split, ablations, external validation, and uncertainty.

Revision direction: Report model settings, validation strategy, failure modes, uncertainty, and experimental confirmation of key predictions.

## statistical_inference_uncertainty_and_replicates

Claim type: `quantitative_difference_or_generalization_claim`

Evidence risk: Quantitative differences may be based on too few replicates, unclear statistics, or unreported uncertainty.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers request replicate counts, error bars, significance tests, variance, and sample representativeness.

Revision direction: Report n, independent repeats, uncertainty intervals, statistical tests, and criteria for excluding measurements.

## data_code_materials_and_reporting_completeness

Claim type: `reproducibility_or_reporting_claim`

Evidence risk: Methods, figures, data, units, source data, code, or materials availability are insufficient for verification or reuse.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers flag missing methodological details, inconsistent figure labels, inaccessible data/code, or ambiguous definitions.

Revision direction: Complete methods, units, captions, data/code availability, materials provenance, and figure-level consistency checks.

## reader_logic_and_figure_narrative

Claim type: `presentation_or_cross_field_accessibility_claim`

Evidence risk: A complex materials story may be hard to follow or may ask readers to infer the evidence chain from scattered figures.

Reviewer concern: Reviewers ask for clearer definitions, schematic logic, figure sequencing, and separation of major and minor claims.

Revision direction: Clarify the central claim, define terms early, align figures with claims, and move secondary details to supplement if needed.
