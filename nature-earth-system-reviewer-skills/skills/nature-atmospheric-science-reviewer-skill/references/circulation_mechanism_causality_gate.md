# Mechanism, causality, and circulation dynamics gate

Use when the manuscript uses causal language about circulation, feedbacks, moisture, energy, radiative, chemical, or mass-budget pathways.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- Mechanism claim → association without closure → causal language is premature → quantify the relevant budget or pathway.
- Circulation response claim → known modes or background forcing not controlled → confounding is possible → test ENSO, NAO, SAM, monsoon, aerosol, or warming controls as relevant.
- Feedback claim → sign and pathway not closed → interpretation may be circular → separate driver, response, and diagnostic evidence.
