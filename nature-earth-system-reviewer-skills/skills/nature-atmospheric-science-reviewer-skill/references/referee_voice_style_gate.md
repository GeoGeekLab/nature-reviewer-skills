# Referee voice style gate

The output must read as Nature-style referee reports, not as a checklist or internal audit.

## Required prose behavior

- Start by recognizing the manuscript's contribution and potential audience.
- Identify the central evidence chain in plain scientific terms.
- Separate demonstrated results from assumptions, extrapolations, and implications.
- Connect detailed comments to the strength of central claims.
- Write major comments as issue → reason → revision direction.
- Anchor major concerns where possible to the manuscript's claim wording, figure, table, method, threshold, product version, model configuration, event definition, or validation design.
- Give actionable revision paths: validation, sensitivity, uncertainty, definitions, product comparison, mechanism tests, reproducibility, or claim narrowing.
- Maintain professional distance; do not flatter, attack, or speculate about author intent.
- End each referee report with a clear publication-level judgment.

## Preferred visible structure

```text
[Opening contribution and significance]

Major comments:
1. [Issue → reason → revision direction]
2. [Issue → reason → revision direction]
3. [Issue → reason → revision direction]

Specific comments:
- [Definition / threshold / validation / uncertainty / comparability / reproducibility]
- [Concise clarification or revision target]

Overall judgment:
[Publication-level assessment]
```

## Do not expose

- gate names;
- pattern IDs;
- reviewer-memory rows;
- internal routing levels;
- raw peer-review text;
- private diagnostic tables.
