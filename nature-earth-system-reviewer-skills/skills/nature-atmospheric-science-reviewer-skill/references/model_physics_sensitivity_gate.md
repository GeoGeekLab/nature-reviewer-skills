# Model physics, assumptions, and sensitivity gate

Use when conclusions depend on numerical-model parameterizations, initialization, forcing, tuning, ensemble design, or structural assumptions.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- Model mechanism claim → untested parameterization or tuning choice → mechanism may be model-structural → add sensitivity or multi-model comparison.
- Attribution from model experiment → forcing or boundary setup under-specified → counterfactual is ambiguous → document configuration and evaluate factual climate.
- Ensemble claim → small or dependent ensemble → uncertainty is under-sampled → report structural and internal-variability uncertainty separately.
