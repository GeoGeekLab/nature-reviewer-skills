# Trend, variability, and signal detection gate

Use when claims depend on trends, variability shifts, forced signals, modes of variability, or regional-to-global inference.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- Trend claim → autocorrelation and endpoint sensitivity ignored → significance is overstated → use effective sample size and endpoint tests.
- Forced-signal claim → internal variability not separated → mechanism may be natural variability → compare ensembles, products, and modes of variability.
- Regional scaling claim → baseline or domain dependence → global implication is not supported → report robustness across baselines and products.
