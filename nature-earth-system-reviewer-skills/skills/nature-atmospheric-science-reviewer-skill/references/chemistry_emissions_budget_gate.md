# Atmospheric chemistry, emissions, and budget closure gate

Use when claims depend on emissions, lifetimes, chemical transformation, transport, deposition, source attribution, or atmospheric budget closure.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- Emissions trend claim → inventory, chemistry, and transport uncertainties not separated → source signal may be confounded → compare inventories and observations.
- Budget closure claim → residual terms or deposition poorly constrained → imbalance is hard to interpret → show budget terms and uncertainty ranges.
- Chemical mechanism claim → co-emitted species or lifetime assumptions omitted → attribution is incomplete → test chemistry and transport sensitivity.
