# Novelty and related-work positioning gate

Use when the manuscript's broad interest depends on how its contribution is positioned relative to existing datasets, models, benchmarks, diagnostics, or process studies. This gate calibrates scope; it should not displace the evidence-chain gates.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- New dataset or product → presented as new atmospheric mechanism → knowledge contribution is overstated → separate dataset novelty from physical inference.
- New AI or model benchmark → compared to weak or non-operational baselines → performance claim is not well positioned → compare with closest relevant baselines and constraints.
- New regional or event analysis → framed as general atmospheric insight → novelty is local rather than general → clarify transferability and closest related work.
