# Observational product and reanalysis gate

Use when claims depend on station data, campaigns, satellite retrievals, radar/lidar/radiosonde observations, aircraft measurements, or reanalysis products.

Claim type → evidence risk → reviewer concern → revision direction

- Product-based atmospheric trend → unaddressed retrieval or assimilation bias → product artefact may mimic signal → compare products and versions.
- Vertical-structure claim → product has limited vertical sensitivity → process level is not resolved → document sensitivity to level, layer, and quality flags.
- Regime-specific process claim → missingness or cloud/aerosol contamination → sample may not represent the claimed regime → quantify screening effects.
