## Reviewer concern patterns distilled from corpus

- **CECO-P0057** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): Mechanistic explanations should be supported by direct evidence or explicitly labelled as hypotheses when only inferred from patterns.
- **CECO-P0058** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): Driver language should distinguish observed association, plausible mechanism, and demonstrated causal pathway.
- **CECO-P0059** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): If multiple mechanisms could produce the observed pattern, the manuscript should test or discuss alternatives.

## Evidence expected before accepting the claim

- direct mechanism evidence
- alternative explanations
- causal language calibration
- pathway tests

## Typical claim-language risk

- using mechanism or driver language without distinguishing hypothesis from evidence
