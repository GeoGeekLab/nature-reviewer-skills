# Review checkpoints matrix

Use this matrix after claim routing. Keep comments focused on central claims and decision-relevant details.

| Checkpoint | Core question | Evidence expected | Major risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novelty | What is new relative to close prior work? | claim-level comparison | established inference reframed as new |
| Evidence calibration | Does wording match evidence strength? | mapped claim-evidence chain | title/abstract exceeds support |
| Decision threshold | Is the weakness fixable by revision or does it undermine the claim? | explicit severity judgment | major gap treated as minor polish |
| Sampling | Does the design support the scale claimed? | sampling frame and coverage | narrow data generalized broadly |
| Measurement | Does the measured variable represent the target process? | validation and limitations | proxy treated as direct evidence |
| Observational products | Are products handled as uncertain evidence? | validation and intercomparison | product treated as ground truth |
| Models | Are assumptions tested? | structure, parameters, sensitivity, validation | conclusion depends on untested assumptions |
| Statistics | Does inference match data structure? | uncertainty, hierarchy, autocorrelation, effect size | fragile significance or threshold claim |
| Climate trends | Are trends separated from artifacts and confounding? | robust windows, alternatives, attribution logic | trend or attribution overclaim |
| Biogeochemistry | Are stocks, fluxes and mechanisms consistent? | mass balance, baselines, pools, uncertainty | mitigation or process claim overstated |
| Land use | Are categories, baselines and histories explicit? | definitions, history, validation | classification implies unsupported ecology |
| Biodiversity | Are diversity and function directly supported? | taxonomic, functional and detection evidence | local or partial data overgeneralized |
| Mechanism | Are alternatives ruled out? | causal or process evidence | correlation written as mechanism |
| Policy | Does application follow from evidence scale? | constraints, trade-offs, uncertainty | broad prescription from weak evidence |
| Reproducibility | Can the result be audited? | data, code, parameters, workflow | central result not reproducible |
| Detail integrity | Do numbers, units, baselines and figures align? | internal consistency checks | small mismatch changes interpretation |
| Communication | Do figures and terms preserve uncertainty? | clear definitions, units, sample sizes | visual or terminology overclaim |
