## Reviewer concern patterns distilled from corpus

- **CECO-P0089** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): Policy, conservation, or management implications should be supported by evidence at the decision-relevant scale and should not exceed the study design.
- **CECO-P0090** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): Applied recommendations should identify trade-offs, boundary conditions, and uncertainty rather than implying universal transferability.
- **CECO-P0091** (abstracted_reviewer_concern): Claims about mitigation, restoration, or protection should distinguish ecological potential from practical implementation.

## Evidence expected before accepting the claim

- decision-scale evidence
- implementation constraints
- uncertainty/trade-offs
- scope-limited recommendations

## Typical claim-language risk

- turning scientific potential into universal management prescription

## Output expectation

When this gate is triggered, write reviewer comments that are claim-specific, evidence-chain aware, and calibrated to the manuscript's actual scope. Prefer explicit tests or revisions over generic requests.

## Additional stress tests

- For restoration, conservation, climate-mitigation or boundary-setting claims, distinguish scientific potential from implementable policy.
- Check decision scale, trade-offs, governance, land tenure, uncertainty, equity/justice assumptions, leakage, permanence and monitoring requirements.
- Avoid allowing strong policy language to substitute for direct evidence or causal identification.
