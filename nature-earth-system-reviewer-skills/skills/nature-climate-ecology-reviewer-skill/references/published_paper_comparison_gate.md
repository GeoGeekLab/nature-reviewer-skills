# Published-paper comparison gate

## Purpose

Use this gate when novelty, significance or interpretation depends on relation to close prior work.

## Trigger signals

- first, novel, unprecedented, global, largest, framework, synthesis;
- similar datasets, models, systems or claims in the reference list;
- omission of directly relevant critiques, reanalyses or alternative explanations;
- claims that a new scale, product, model or dataset changes interpretation.

## Review standard

Compare at the claim level, not the topic level. Ask what the manuscript establishes that the closest studies did not establish.

## Comparison axes

- Claim: same conclusion, narrower conclusion, or genuinely new inference.
- Data: new observations, broader coverage, better validation, or repackaged source.
- Method: new design, model, experiment, synthesis or only new implementation.
- Scale: spatial, temporal, taxonomic, ecosystem or scenario extension.
- Mechanism: new process evidence or only new association.
- Uncertainty: stronger validation, sensitivity and robustness.
- Interpretation: changed ecological, climatic or applied implication.

## Evidence expected

- Closest-paper comparison.
- Clear statement of what is genuinely new.
- Engagement with known limitations or critiques when relevant.
- Explanation of why the new evidence changes interpretation.

## Major risk

A manuscript may appear novel because of scale, framing or terminology while repeating an established or contested inference.
