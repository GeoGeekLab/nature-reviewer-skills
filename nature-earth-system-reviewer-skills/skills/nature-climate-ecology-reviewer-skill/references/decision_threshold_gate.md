# Decision threshold gate

## Purpose

Separate fixable presentation issues from evidence gaps that affect suitability for publication.

## Decision levels

| Level | Meaning | Typical recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Evidence is sound; local clarification is needed. | minor revision |
| Major | Central claim may stand, but requires clearer validation, uncertainty, comparison or wording. | major revision |
| Substantial reanalysis | Central claim depends on missing tests, weak controls, unsupported generalization or unstable modelling. | major revision with reanalysis |
| Not supported | The main conclusion is not established by the current evidence chain. | reject or resubmit after redesign |

## Escalation triggers

Escalate when the manuscript:

- claims novelty without showing what close prior work did not establish;
- treats a proxy, product or model output as direct process evidence;
- generalizes beyond the sampled sites, years, taxa, products or scenarios;
- relies on one model, product, threshold or baseline without sensitivity analysis;
- presents correlation, co-occurrence or temporal alignment as mechanism;
- converts exposure, association or potential into impact, causality or policy readiness;
- lacks data, code or parameter detail needed to audit the central result.

## Review standard

State the decision consequence plainly. Do not hide a central evidence gap behind soft wording.
