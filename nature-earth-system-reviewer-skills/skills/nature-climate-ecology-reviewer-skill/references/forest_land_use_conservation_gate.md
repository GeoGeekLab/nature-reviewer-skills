# Forest, land-use and conservation gate

## Purpose

Use this gate for claims about forests, land use, restoration, degradation, protection,
management or conservation outcomes.

## Trigger signals

forest, land use, restoration, regeneration, degradation, protection, conservation,
management, plantation, fragmentation, habitat change.

## Core checks

- Are ecosystem and land-use categories defined clearly?
- Are baselines, histories and reference states appropriate?
- Are classification or field estimates independently validated?
- Are site selection, governance, access and leakage considered?
- Are biodiversity, carbon and management outcomes separated?

## Evidence expected

Definitions, sampling logic, independent validation, land-use history, uncertainty and
confounder analysis.

## Major risk

Land-cover change, protection status or management label is interpreted as ecological
recovery, climate benefit or conservation effectiveness without sufficient evidence.
