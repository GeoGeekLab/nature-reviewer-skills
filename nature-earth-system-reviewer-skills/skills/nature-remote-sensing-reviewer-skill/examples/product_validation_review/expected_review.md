Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):
The product could fill an important observational gap for monsoon-region surface water, especially if it improves spatial detail and seasonal coverage. The validation evidence is not yet sufficient to support the product-level claim.

The authors should describe the in situ match-up design, including spatial buffers, temporal windows, field-label uncertainty, and whether field sites are independent of algorithm development. Validation should be stratified by water permanence, vegetation cover, turbidity, shadow, and mixed-pixel conditions.

The manuscript should also compare the proposed product with established surface-water products or algorithms in this domain without inventing unsupported priority claims. Disagreements should be analyzed by region and condition, and uncertainty should be propagated into area estimates.
