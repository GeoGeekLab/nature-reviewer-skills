Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):
The manuscript addresses a relevant water-cycle monitoring problem and could provide a useful regional evapotranspiration product. The current evidence chain, however, does not yet establish whether the satellite product is reliable across the dryland conditions where the strongest interpretation is made.

Methods, validation paragraph: The validation design appears to use tower sites from the same region used for model tuning. The authors should clarify whether validation sites are independent of calibration and should report performance for withheld sites or spatially blocked regions.

Methods, compositing paragraph: The monthly compositing strategy may interact with cloud-free sampling. Please report valid-observation coverage and test whether the trend is sensitive to compositing choices.

Results/discussion paragraph: The groundwater interpretation should be calibrated unless the manuscript provides independent evidence linking the satellite trend to groundwater depletion rather than climate variability or land-cover change.
