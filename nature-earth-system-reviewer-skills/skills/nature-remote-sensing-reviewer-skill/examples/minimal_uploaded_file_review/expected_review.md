Editorial assessment: the manuscript may require major revision before its present claims can be evaluated at Nature level.

Reviewer Reports on the Initial Version:
Referees' comments:

Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):

The manuscript addresses a problem that could be of interest to readers working with remote-sensing products. I am less sure, however, that the current material establishes the product claim. The validation information available in the submitted file is too thin to judge whether the retrieval is reliable outside the calibration examples.

Referee #2 (Remarks to the Author):

Here I have a more practical concern. The manuscript refers to uncertainty estimates, but I do not see enough information on how these uncertainties are calibrated or propagated into the reported maps. If the authors intend the maps to support inference rather than illustration, this needs to be made explicit.

Specific comments:
1. Please avoid describing reference observations as ground truth unless their measurement support and uncertainty are defined.
2. If no line numbers are supplied, section or figure references should be used instead.
