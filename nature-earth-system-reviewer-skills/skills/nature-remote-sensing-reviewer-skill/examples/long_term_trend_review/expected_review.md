Referee #2 (Remarks to the Author):
The manuscript addresses a long-term environmental-change question with clear potential significance. The evidence for the trend and its interpretation needs stronger statistical and product-consistency support.

The trend analysis should account for temporal autocorrelation, start- and end-year sensitivity, missing observations, and possible sensor or product transitions across the 35-year record. The mapped significance results should state how multiple testing and spatial dependence are handled.

The interpretation of "ecosystem recovery" is stronger than the evidence described here. A vegetation-index trend may be consistent with recovery, but alternative explanations such as climate variability, land management, sensor artifacts, or compositing changes should be considered. The claim should be calibrated unless independent ecological evidence is provided.
