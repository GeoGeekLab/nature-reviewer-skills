Referees' comments:

Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):

This review is limited to the abstract-level information provided. The abstract addresses an important observational problem and suggests a potentially useful remote-sensing product with broad relevance. My foremost concern is that the abstract makes strong claims without providing enough information to assess the methods, validation design, uncertainty propagation, figures, or supplementary material.

I am not convinced from the abstract alone that the claimed temporal trend, spatial generality, or driver interpretation can be supported. The authors should clarify the validation data, spatial and temporal independence of testing, uncertainty in change estimates, and the basis for separating environmental drivers from retrieval artifacts.

Minor comments:
1. Terms such as "unprecedented", "demonstrates", or "dominant driver" should be calibrated unless the full manuscript provides strong supporting evidence.
2. Because only the abstract is available, no line-specific comments can be made.
