# Contribution Assessment Gate

Assess contribution before critique. Identify novelty, conceptual advance, technical advance, broader relevance, and whether the contribution is coupled to evidence.

Novelty may come from a new problem, data source, regional scale, temporal span, observation angle, or product. Conceptual advance requires a change in understanding of an Earth-system process, observation mechanism, or environmental-change interpretation. Technical advance requires demonstrated improvement in algorithms, sensor fusion, retrieval, error modeling, validation design, or reproducibility. Broader relevance requires evidence that the work matters beyond the case study for policy, resource management, climate, ecology, hydrology, hazards, or cross-regional application.

A manuscript may have a real contribution despite requiring major revision. State that value clearly, then explain the evidence gap. An important topic is not automatically a Nature-level contribution. If the work is useful but narrow, say so and calibrate the journal-level claim.
