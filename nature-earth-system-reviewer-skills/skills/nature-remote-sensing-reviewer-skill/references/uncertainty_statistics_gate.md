# Uncertainty and Statistics Gate

Review uncertainty propagation from inputs through retrievals, models, products, trends, and interpretation. Ask for confidence intervals or credible intervals where quantitative claims require them.

Trend claims should address trend tests, autocorrelation, temporal dependence, breakpoint or start/end-year sensitivity, and uncertainty in product consistency. Spatial inference should address spatial dependence, pseudoreplication, and multiple testing across maps, regions, or thresholds.

Model claims should address blocked validation, temporal leakage, spatial leakage, model calibration, baseline comparisons, ablations, and sensitivity tests.
