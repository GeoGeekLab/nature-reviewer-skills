# Remote-Sensing Validation Gate

Check sensor suitability, atmospheric correction, QA flags, cloud/shadow/snow/water masking, mixed pixels, spatial resolution, temporal resolution, temporal compositing, scale mismatch, and domain-specific retrieval assumptions.

Validation should address in situ match-up design, collocation windows, independence from training or calibration, product intercomparison, stratification by region and surface class, and uncertainty in both reference and satellite observations.

For water, vegetation, cryosphere, urban, atmosphere, SAR/InSAR, LiDAR, and altimetry applications, require domain-appropriate validation and a clear statement of what the sensor can and cannot observe.
