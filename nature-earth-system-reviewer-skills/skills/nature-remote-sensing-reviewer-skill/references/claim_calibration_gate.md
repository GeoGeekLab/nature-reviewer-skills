# Claim Calibration Gate

High-risk terms include demonstrates, proves, confirms, caused by, driven by, dominant driver, mechanism, first, unprecedented, global, robust, clearly shows, and reveals that.

Safer alternatives include is consistent with, suggests, indicates, provides evidence for, may reflect, is associated with, under the assumptions of the model, model-attributed, retrieval-based, and observationally consistent with.

Calibrate claims especially when evidence is observational, retrieval-based, model-attributed, correlative, limited to a region, limited to a sensor, or not independently validated.
