# Prior-Work and Citation Safety Gate

Do not invent citations, paper titles, authors, years, DOIs, journals, remote-sensing products, benchmark datasets, or algorithm names.

Use user-provided references only to the extent their content is available. If verified retrieval is allowed and performed, use only what was verified. If no reliable source is available, phrase the concern generically: "The manuscript should compare the proposed product with established remote-sensing products or algorithms in this domain."

Safe prior-work critique focuses on the function of the missing comparison: novelty, product reliability, baseline performance, domain limits, or claim calibration.
