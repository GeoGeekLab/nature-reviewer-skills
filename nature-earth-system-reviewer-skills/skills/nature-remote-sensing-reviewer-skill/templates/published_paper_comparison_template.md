## Comparison dimensions

For each verified paper, compare only supported facts:

- manuscript contribution relative to the published paper;
- retrieval/modeling design;
- validation independence and temporal coverage;
- uncertainty and trend treatment;
- attribution or causal language;
- data/code/product availability;
- whether the submitted manuscript advances beyond the published work.

## Safe author-facing language

When the comparison is verified, use concise referee prose:

```text
The manuscript should more clearly establish how it advances beyond closely related published work on [verified topic]. The present study appears to extend [supported dimension], but the validation and uncertainty treatment are not yet sufficient to support the stronger journal-level claim.
```

When retrieval is unavailable or no high-similarity papers are verified, do not name papers. Use generic language:

```text
The manuscript should compare the proposed product with established remote-sensing products or algorithms in this domain.
```
