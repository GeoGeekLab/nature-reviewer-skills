# Sea Ice Ocean Atmosphere

Ice-free thresholds, momentum, stratification, biological pump, moving boundaries, atmospheric rivers and regimes.

## Internal questions

- **Ice-free threshold is not explicit:** An ice-free Arctic, ice-free day, or seasonal loss is discussed without a concentration, extent, area, region, or duration threshold. Resolution: Define the metric and threshold before analysis and report sensitivity to accepted alternatives.
- **Wind stress transfer ignores ice mechanics:** Surface stress or ocean spin-up is linked to sea-ice loss without resolving internal ice stress, concentration, thickness, drag, and seasonal regime. Resolution: Diagnose the full momentum budget and test sensitivity to drag and rheology choices.
- **Sea-ice effect on stratification lacks competing freshwater controls:** Winter stratification or carbon uptake is attributed to sea ice without separating brine rejection, meltwater, river input, precipitation, mixing, and advection. Resolution: Close the salinity and mixing budget and test whether the sea-ice pathway is uniquely supported.
- **Biological-pump response is inferred from proxies alone:** Export or carbon sequestration is inferred from production, community composition, sediment traps, or model diagnostics without closing remineralization and export pathways. Resolution: Measure or constrain export efficiency, remineralization, depth horizon, and seasonal integration; narrow the claim if only production changes are shown.
- **Marginal ice zone migration confounds trend:** Ecological or oceanographic differences are attributed to ice decline although sampling follows a moving ice edge or habitat boundary. Resolution: Use a coordinate system relative to both geography and ice edge, and separate habitat relocation from temporal change.
- **Atmospheric-river detection is algorithm-dependent:** Future atmospheric-river frequency or impact is assessed with one detection threshold or tracking algorithm in a changing moisture climate. Resolution: Compare absolute and relative thresholds, multiple algorithms, and impact-based diagnostics; report algorithm dependence.
- **Sea-ice state change inferred without process consistency:** A new state or regime is declared from low extent alone without consistent evidence in thickness, seasonality, circulation, persistence, or restoring behavior. Resolution: Define the state vector, test persistence and multivariate consistency, and compare with internal-variability ensembles.
- **Ocean circulation proxy is non-unique:** Sediment, grain-size, geochemical, or water-mass proxies are interpreted as current strength without excluding source, productivity, preservation, or frontal-position effects. Resolution: Use a multi-proxy calibration and model/modern constraints, and distinguish current strength from related water-mass changes.

Use only when relevant to a central claim. Do not expose this gate name or pattern IDs in the final referee report.
