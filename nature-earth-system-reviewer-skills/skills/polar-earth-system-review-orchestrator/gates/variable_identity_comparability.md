# Variable Identity Comparability

Sea ice, glacier, ice sheet, permafrost, hydrologic, carbon and proxy variables must not be conflated.

## Internal questions

- **Sea-ice metrics are interchanged:** Sea-ice concentration, area, extent, thickness, volume, age, and duration are discussed as if they measured the same system property. Resolution: Define every metric and threshold, preserve units and denominators, and align the claim with the measured quantity.
- **Elevation change equated with mass change:** Surface-elevation change is interpreted as ice mass change without separating density, firn compaction, snow variability, floating geometry, or dynamic effects. Resolution: State the density and firn assumptions, propagate them, distinguish grounded and floating ice, and compare an independent mass estimate.
- **Glacier retreat equated with mass loss:** Terminus position, area loss, calving-front retreat, frontal ablation, thinning, and total mass balance are conflated. Resolution: Use precise glacier-change variables, report geometry and mass components separately, and revise causal language.
- **Permafrost indicators are conflated:** Ground temperature, active-layer thickness, permafrost extent, ground-ice loss, thermokarst, subsidence, and carbon release are treated as interchangeable thaw indicators. Resolution: Define the measured state variable, depth and season; avoid translating one indicator into another without a validated model.
- **Surface mass balance and total balance mixed:** Accumulation and surface ablation are combined conceptually with ice discharge, basal melt, or calving when interpreting ice-sheet or glacier mass balance. Resolution: Report each budget component and sign convention, identify residual terms, and use total mass balance only when all relevant components are included.
- **Proxy target changes across calibration:** A proxy is calibrated to one environmental variable but interpreted as another, such as temperature to circulation strength or sediment composition to storminess. Resolution: Specify the mechanistic proxy chain, test competing controls, and calibrate against the claimed target at matching scale.
- **Products with different definitions are merged:** Time series from products using different masks, thresholds, sensors, retrieval targets, or version conventions are concatenated without harmonization. Resolution: Document definitions and versions, quantify inter-product offsets, and construct a homogenized record or analyze products separately.
- **Freshwater, meltwater, and runoff are mixed:** Ice melt, snowmelt, liquid precipitation, subglacial discharge, runoff, and net freshwater flux are used interchangeably. Resolution: Partition sources and pathways, state the control volume, and use terminology consistent with the measured or modeled flux.

Use only when relevant to a central claim. Do not expose this gate name or pattern IDs in the final referee report.
