# Polar corpus distillation report

## Corpus

- Public peer-review files: **34**
- Nature Portfolio peer-review files: **24** (15 *Nature Communications*, 7 *Communications Earth & Environment*, 1 *Nature*, 1 *Nature Climate Change*)
- The Cryosphere Discussions referee comments: **10**
- Official standards, product guides, research-priority or treaty sources: **8**
- Peer-review pages inspected: **850**
- Downloaded peer-review bytes hashed: **47,518,365**
- Final non-verbatim reviewer patterns: **104** across **13** gates

## Coverage

The corpus covers Arctic and Antarctic sea ice, ice sheets, glaciers, ice shelves, grounding lines, permafrost, polar atmosphere and ocean, ecology and biogeochemistry, remote sensing, paleoclimate proxies and ice cores. Official sources add variable definitions, measurement and product practices, Indigenous/community research responsibility, and Antarctic protected-area/permit reporting checks.

## Distillation protocol

1. Verify each peer-review PDF visually as a public review/comment file.
2. Record title, journal, DOI or discussion record, public URL, SHA-256, byte size and page count.
3. Extract recurring scientific failure modes and requested evidence without retaining reviewer wording.
4. Merge case-specific comments into transferable claim-risk-gate-revision abstractions.
5. Add official standards only where they define variables, methods, product caveats or research responsibilities.
6. Deduplicate semantically overlapping patterns and require an actionable revision direction.
7. Validate source IDs, schema, counts, checksums and search behavior.

## Copyright and privacy boundary

Raw peer-review PDFs are not distributed in this repository. Anonymous-reviewer identities are not inferred. The package contains only bibliographic/source metadata, hashes, aggregate statistics and non-verbatim abstractions. Users should access original public files from the source publishers under the terms shown there.

## Scientific limitation

Corpus breadth does not establish expert-equivalent review performance. The patterns are evidence prompts. Independent polar experts, blinded manuscripts, negative controls, routing tests and prospective evaluation remain necessary.
