# Chemistry claim-evidence calibration gate

## Purpose

Calibrate chemical claim strength to the evidence actually demonstrated.

## High-risk signals

- A local result is framed as a general principle.
- A successful synthesis, catalyst, probe, or material is presented as mechanistically explained.
- A best-case example anchors the central claim.
- A practical, industrial, biomedical, energy, or sustainability implication is made from laboratory-scale evidence.

## Reviewer translation

Ask whether the manuscript demonstrates the exact claim it makes. If not, require narrower wording, additional controls, broader scope, mechanistic discrimination, or explicit limits.
