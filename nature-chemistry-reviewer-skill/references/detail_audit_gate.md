# Detail audit gate

## Purpose

Catch details that affect claim auditability but do not always rise to a major conceptual flaw.

## Common issues

- Vague descriptors such as robust, universal, green, efficient, hierarchical, stable, programmable, or selective.
- Figure labels that do not show conditions, sample sizes, error bars, or controls.
- Inconsistent compound names, catalyst labels, units, charge states, stereochemistry, or abbreviations.
- Supplementary information does not align with main-text claims.

## Revision direction

Request operational definitions, quantitative thresholds, clearer figures, consistent nomenclature, and direct figure-to-claim alignment.
