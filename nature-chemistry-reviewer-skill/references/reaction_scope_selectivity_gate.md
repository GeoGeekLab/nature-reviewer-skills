# Reaction scope and selectivity gate

## Purpose

Evaluate whether synthesis and reaction-method claims are supported by substrate scope, selectivity analysis, and limitations.

## Audit dimensions

- Electronic variation.
- Steric variation.
- Functional-group tolerance.
- Heteroatom and heterocycle coverage.
- Regio-, chemo-, stereo-, and enantioselectivity.
- Scale, isolation, purification, side products, mass balance.
- Failed substrates and boundary cases.

## Revision direction

Require a scope design that tests the claimed generality. Require explicit limitations and consistent reporting of conversion, isolated yield, selectivity, and conditions.
