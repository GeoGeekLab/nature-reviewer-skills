# Computational chemistry and model-assumption gate

## Purpose

Evaluate DFT, quantum chemistry, molecular dynamics, microkinetic modelling, cheminformatics, retrosynthesis, and AI/ML chemistry claims.

## Risks

- Simplified model chemistry is mismatched to experiment.
- Solvent, counterion, surface, defect, concentration, or dynamic effects are omitted.
- One functional, basis set, force field, slab, cluster, or conformer is used without sensitivity analysis.
- Retrospective AI benchmarks are framed as prospective chemical prediction.
- Uncertainty and out-of-domain behaviour are not reported.

## Revision direction

Require model justification, sensitivity analysis, competing pathways or models, prospective validation when claims are predictive, and clear boundaries around computational inference.
