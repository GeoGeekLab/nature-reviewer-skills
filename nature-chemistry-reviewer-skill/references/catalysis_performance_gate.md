# Catalysis and performance gate

## Purpose

Audit catalytic activity, selectivity, stability, product balance, and comparability.

## Core checks

- Are performance metrics normalized to chemically meaningful denominators?
- Are turnover number, turnover frequency, Faradaic efficiency, quantum yield, site density, absorbed photons, or active surface area reported when relevant?
- Are product balances and calibration curves complete?
- Is stability tested long enough and under realistic stress?
- Is post-catalysis reconstruction, leaching, poisoning, or deactivation assessed?

## Reviewer translation

If apparent activity may reflect loading, surface area, morphology, transport, illumination, current, or product accounting, request matched controls and intrinsic metrics.
