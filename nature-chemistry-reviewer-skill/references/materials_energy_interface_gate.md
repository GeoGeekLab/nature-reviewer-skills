# Materials, energy, and interface chemistry gate

## Purpose

Evaluate chemistry-centred materials, energy, polymer, nanostructure, interface, and environmental manuscripts.

## Risks

- Performance is attributed to chemical design when morphology, transport, device architecture, additives, defects, or interface evolution may dominate.
- Ex situ characterization is used to assign operating-state chemistry.
- Practical relevance is claimed without realistic stability, process, feedstock, safety, or comparator metrics.

## Revision direction

Require matched materials and device controls, operando or in situ evidence when feasible, post-operation characterization, and calibrated practical claims.
