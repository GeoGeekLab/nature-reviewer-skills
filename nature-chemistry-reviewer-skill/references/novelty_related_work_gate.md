# Novelty and related-work positioning gate

## Purpose

Test whether the chemistry contribution is novel relative to the closest reaction class, catalyst family, molecular design, analytical method, computational workflow, or neighbouring subfield.

## Common risks

- Existing concepts are renamed as new paradigms.
- Adjacent fields are not cited.
- Benchmark comparisons are absent or favourable.
- The manuscript compares against weak or outdated baselines.

## Revision direction

Require a precise novelty statement, closest-precedent comparison, and explicit explanation of what is new: mechanism, substrate class, selectivity, catalyst architecture, analytical capability, computational prediction, or application boundary.
