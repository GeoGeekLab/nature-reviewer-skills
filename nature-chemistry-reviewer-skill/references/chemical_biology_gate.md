# Chemical biology and biointerface gate

## Purpose

Audit manuscripts where chemical probes, inhibitors, binders, biomolecular dynamics, labelling reactions, or biointerface chemistry support biological claims.

## Risks

- Biological readout is treated as direct evidence of chemical engagement.
- Off-target chemistry, toxicity, uptake, localization, metabolism, or nonspecific binding is not excluded.
- Probe specificity is inferred from a single molecule or assay.
- Structural or spectroscopic data are overextended to biological function.

## Revision direction

Require orthogonal probes, inactive analogues, rescue experiments, dose response, target engagement, localization controls, and cautious interpretation of biological readouts.
