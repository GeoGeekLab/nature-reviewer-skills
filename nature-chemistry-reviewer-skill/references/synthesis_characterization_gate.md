# Synthesis, characterization, and analytical evidence gate

## Purpose

Assess whether key molecules, intermediates, catalysts, polymers, materials, or products are identified, quantified, and pure enough to support the conclusions.

## Evidence classes

- NMR, HRMS, MS, GC, LC, HPLC, elemental analysis.
- X-ray crystallography, powder diffraction, electron diffraction.
- XPS, XAS, EXAFS, XANES, EPR, Raman, FTIR, UV-vis.
- Chromatograms, calibration curves, standards, limits of detection.
- Batch reproducibility, impurity analysis, residual metal, counterion, solvent, morphology, and phase purity.

## Reviewer translation

If structure, identity, purity, or quantification is underdetermined, require orthogonal characterization and clear uncertainty reporting.
