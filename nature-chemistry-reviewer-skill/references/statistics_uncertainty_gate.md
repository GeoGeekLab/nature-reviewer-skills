# Statistical inference and uncertainty gate

## Purpose

Assess whether chemical measurements, fitted parameters, performance comparisons, and model outputs include enough uncertainty information to support the conclusions.

## Checks

- Replicate type and number are clear.
- Error bars and confidence intervals match the experimental design.
- Fitting models include residuals, confidence intervals, and parameter sensitivity.
- Small differences are not overinterpreted.
- Uncertainty is propagated through calibration, quantification, and derived metrics.

## Revision direction

Require replicate definitions, raw and processed data, effect-size thresholds, fitting uncertainty, and uncertainty propagation.
