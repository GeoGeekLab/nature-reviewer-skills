# Mechanism and causality gate

## Purpose

Separate mechanistic demonstration from plausible rationalization.

## High-risk signals

- Mechanism is inferred from performance correlation.
- A single pathway is presented without excluding alternatives.
- Active sites or intermediates are assigned from ex situ or indirect evidence alone.
- Computation is used only to support the preferred pathway.
- Kinetic, isotope, trapping, or perturbation evidence is absent.

## Revision direction

Require direct or discriminating evidence where feasible. Otherwise require tempered language and explicit acknowledgement of plausible alternative mechanisms.
