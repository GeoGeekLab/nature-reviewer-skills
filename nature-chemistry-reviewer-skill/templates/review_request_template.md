# Review request template

Use nature-chemistry-reviewer-skill-v1.0 to review the manuscript below as a Nature-style chemistry referee.

Please produce 3 independent referee reports in the following structure:

```text
Reviewer Reports on the Initial Version

Referees' comments:

Referee #1 (Remarks to the Author):
...

Referee #2 (Remarks to the Author):
...

Referee #3 (Remarks to the Author):
...
```

Focus on claim strength, closest chemistry literature, chemical evidence, controls, characterization, analytical validation, mechanism, uncertainty and reproducibility. Anchor major concerns to concrete manuscript evidence when possible: a figure, scheme, table, spectrum, chromatogram, method, reaction condition, computational setting, dataset split, threshold or short quoted claim.

Do not expose internal gates, routing tables, reviewer-memory IDs or pattern IDs.
