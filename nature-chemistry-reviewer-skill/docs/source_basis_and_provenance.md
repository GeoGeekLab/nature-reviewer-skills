# Source basis and provenance

This skill was developed from a local best-effort corpus of public Nature Portfolio peer-review files related to chemistry and chemical sciences.

## Corpus summary

- Date range: 2024-01-01 to 2026-06-25.
- Downloaded peer-review files: 20.
- Candidate records: 38.
- Failure/excluded records: 18.
- Journals: Communications Chemistry, Nature, Nature Catalysis, Nature Chemistry, Nature Communications, Nature Synthesis.

## Inclusion principle

Articles were included when they were high-core or medium-high related to chemistry and had an explicitly public Peer Review File, Peer Reviewer Reports, or Transparent Peer Review File.

## Exclusion principle

Records were excluded when they had no public peer-review file, were low-related, unrelated, non-research article types, duplicate DOI records, failed links, or fell outside the defined scope.

## Public repository boundary

The public skill repository does not include raw peer-review PDFs or long reviewer comments. It includes abstracted reviewer-memory patterns, non-verbatim unit metadata, corpus provenance, tests, and validation scripts.
