# Evaluation protocol

A credible claim of review quality requires expert-labelled manuscripts, not only package tests.

## Required datasets

Use accepted, rejected, and deliberately perturbed manuscripts. Include clean negative controls. Remove confidential and identifying information unless use is authorized. Gold concerns should include an issue identifier, severity, evidence anchors, acceptable alternative phrasings, and whether the concern is essential.

## Required metrics

- Essential-issue recall.
- Concern precision and false-positive rate.
- Severity macro-F1 or weighted agreement.
- Evidence-anchor coverage and anchor validity.
- Unsupported-citation rate.
- Duplicate-concern rate across panel members.
- Run-to-run stability.
- Human usefulness and actionability scores.

Report per-domain confidence intervals. Do not pool domains in a way that hides failure in a low-volume specialty.

## Human labelling

Use at least two independent domain experts. Record adjudication and Cohen's kappa or Krippendorff's alpha where applicable. Keep evaluation manuscripts separate from pattern-development sources.
