# Synthetic manuscript excerpt

## Claim

We developed a visible-light coupling that provides a broad range of heteroaryl products in consistently high yield. Across 24 substrates, all products were obtained in 78–96% yield, demonstrating broad functional-group tolerance and synthetic robustness.

## Scope quantification

Reaction mixtures were analysed by reverse-phase HPLC with UV detection at 254 nm. Product yield was calculated as the product peak area divided by the summed integrated area of all detected peaks at 254 nm.

The substrate set includes electron-rich anisole derivatives, electron-poor pyridines, fused heteroarenes, and products containing additional conjugated chromophores.

No compound-specific UV response factors were measured. Authentic calibration standards were not used for the scope table. Five representative products were isolated for structural characterization, but isolated yields were not reported in the scope figure.

## Interpretation

Because all HPLC area-derived yields exceed 78%, we conclude that the transformation is uniformly high yielding across electronically and structurally diverse substrates.
