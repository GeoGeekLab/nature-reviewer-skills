## The scientific problem

A long-term vegetation trend is estimated from two satellite sensors. The manuscript interprets the combined record as a physical ecosystem trend, but the sensor transition occurs near the apparent breakpoint and no overlap-period harmonization or sensor-effect sensitivity test is shown.
