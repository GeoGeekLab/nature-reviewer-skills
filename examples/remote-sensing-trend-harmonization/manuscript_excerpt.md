# Synthetic manuscript excerpt

## Claim

We reconstructed annual peak-season vegetation greenness across the Central Plateau from 2001 to 2025 and found a pronounced acceleration after 2014. We interpret this acceleration as evidence that recent warming has increased regional vegetation productivity.

## Data and processing

For 2001–2013, annual peak greenness was calculated from Sensor A at 250 m resolution. From 2014 onward, the analysis used Sensor B at 300 m resolution because Sensor A coverage became incomplete. Both products were converted to the same vegetation index, reprojected to a common 300 m grid, and averaged over the study region.

The combined time series increased by 0.0018 index units per year before 2014 and by 0.0061 index units per year after 2014. A segmented linear regression identified a breakpoint in 2014 (p < 0.01).

## Interpretation

Because the breakpoint coincides with a period of rapid regional warming, we conclude that climate warming caused a substantial acceleration in vegetation greening after 2014.
